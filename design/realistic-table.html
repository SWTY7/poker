Realistic table: structure, layout and texture

Taken from your two screenshots: a wooden tabletop under a padded rail, name plates with a portrait on the left and three lines of text, chip piles on the felt in front of each seat, and a larger dock for you with fanned cards. On the phone, the table tilts away from you in perspective and your cards and chips sit big at the bottom. Colours stay in the current Realistic palette (green felt, brass, dark leather). No photos or realistic people.

Avatars: pick one of three

All three are drawn in code (SVG), so they work offline and stay tiny. Each player gets the same figure every time, picked from their name. Shown below at the size used in the seat plate.

What changes in the app. Realistic theme only; Classic stays as it is. The table gets the wooden floor, padded rail and felt texture (in landscape and phone layouts), the seat becomes a plate with a portrait, the dealer button and bets become chips on the felt, and the avatar is replaced by the direction you choose. The seat component keeps the same fields, so the game logic is untouched.
